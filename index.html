🌍 Greenhouse Gas Emissions Dashboard

Emissions across countries, regions & sectors • 1970-2024

This dashboard provides insights into global greenhouse gas emissions data. How are global emissions evolving and how far are we to reach the Paris Climate Goals? Explore emissions trends, compare countries and regions, and analyze sector-specific contributions. Use the year slider to navigate through different time periods and click on countries to see detailed breakdowns.

The unit used is MtCO₂e (or respectively tCO₂e), which are CO₂ equivalent emissions, a standard unit to measure greenhouse gas emissions by converting different gases into the equivalent amount of CO₂ based on their global warming potential.
